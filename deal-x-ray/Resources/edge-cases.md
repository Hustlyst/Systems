# Deal X-ray edge cases

These are the situations where the standard method gives a wrong answer, and what to do in each.

## A renewal or an upsell to an existing customer

The economic buyer and the paper process are often already known from the first contract. Check whether either has changed. A new finance leader, a new procurement policy or a budget cut since the last signature puts both back to Assumed.

## A deal sold through a partner or reseller

The seller rarely meets the economic buyer directly. Grade the economic buyer as Proven only when the partner has named them and described what they need, in writing. Add one task: confirm with the partner who owns the paper process and how long it takes.

## A very small deal

Below a few thousand in value, the full eight points are more than the deal needs. Grade pain, champion and paper process only, and say so at the top of the report. The rest stays "Not judged".

## A large deal with a buying committee

When five or more people are involved, grade the champion separately for each person who might fill the role, and list who blocks. A committee deal with no proven champion is pipeline, whatever stage the CRM shows.

## Only the seller's notes, with no transcript

Notes are the seller's summary, so almost nothing in them can be Proven. Only lines the notes mark as the buyer's own words can be Proven. Say clearly at the top of the report that the run used notes only, and add a task to record or take verbatim notes on the next call.

## The buyer went quiet

When there has been no reply for two weeks or more, nothing new can be proven. Grade as before, then move the verdict down one level and add a task to send one short message that asks for a yes or no on the next step.

## A champion who changed jobs

When the champion leaves or moves teams, grade the champion as Missing at once, even if the deal looked strong. A deal that has lost its champion is pipeline until someone new is proven.

## A buyer who asks for a discount early

A discount request before the economic buyer is proven usually means the contact is trying to make the case internally without a business case. Treat it as a sign that metrics and the economic buyer are weak. Do not grade it as progress.
