# Value Pricing templates

## The value case, for the reader only

```text
VALUE CASE: [client, described] | [date]

COST OF THE PROBLEM TODAY
[calculation, step by step, with each figure's source]
= [low] to [high] a year

VALUE OF SOLVING IT
[calculation, step by step]
= [low] to [high] a year

THEIR ALTERNATIVES
Hire someone:      [cost a year]
Another supplier:  [cost, if known]
Do nothing:        [what it costs them over a year]

ASSUMPTIONS TO CONFIRM
1. [assumption] | low [x], high [y] | source: [where from]
   Ask: "[question]"

FLOOR (private): [amount]
```

## The three options, for the proposal

```text
OPTION 1: [name]                                   [price]
The result: [one line, in the client's words]
Includes: [list]
Not included: [list]
Timeline: [weeks]

OPTION 2: [name]                                   [price]
...

OPTION 3: [name]                                   [price]
...
```

## The price conversation

```text
OPENING, UNDER TWO MINUTES
1. The cost of the problem, in their words and numbers.
2. Option 3, then option 2, then option 1.
3. One question: "Which of these fits where you want to be?"

LIKELY OBJECTIONS
"[objection]"
  Answer: [calm answer that keeps the price and changes the scope
  if needed]
```

## A logged proposal

```text
- [ ] Log the outcome of the [client] proposal (due YYYY-MM-DD)
```
